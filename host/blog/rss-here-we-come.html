We now have an RSS feed

I've recently finished implementing an RSS generator in C. I broke the Geneva convention multiple times while implementing it. The horrors are incomprehensible! Seriously, I dare you to take a look at its source code :D. It definitely isn't the cleanest implementation of an RSS generator, but it gets the job done, and that makes me happy.

If you want to you view the latest news of Vietexa in your RSS feed, you have to add rss.vietexa.com to your favourite RSS feed viewer app. Once added you will be able to see our posts without having to check our site all the time.

Okay, so now we have an RSS generator, big whoop. Is anyone going to use it? I have no idea, but I do know that it doesn't hurt having it regardless.
